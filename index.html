Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the Expense Tracker Application (the "Service").

1. Information Collection and Use

We collect and use your data to provide and improve the Service. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Personal Data

While using our Service, we may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information to personalize your experience and authenticate your identity. This includes:

  • Email address
  • First name and last name
  • Profile picture URL (obtained via Google Sign-In / OAuth)

Financial and App Data

As a core part of the Service, we collect and store the financial data you voluntarily enter, including:

  • Transaction records (amounts, dates, categories, accounts)
  • Expense categories, accounts, cashbook entries, and piggy bank savings goals
  • Lend and borrow records (person name, amount, dates, and settlement status)
  • App preferences and settings

Local Storage and Biometric Data

  • Local Storage: A copy of your data is stored locally on your device to enable offline access and fast performance. This data is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Biometric Lock: If enabled, biometric authentication is processed entirely on-device by your operating system. We do not collect, store, or transmit biometric data.

2. Third-Party Services

We integrate with third-party services to facilitate authentication and backups:

  • Google Sign-In: Used to securely authenticate your account. We only access your name, email address, and profile picture URL.
  • Google Drive: Used optionally to back up and restore your app data. Backups are stored in your own Google Drive account under your control.

3. Data Deletion and Retention

We retain your personal and financial data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly through the Application settings, or by contacting us at the email address below.

4. Security and Global Operations

We protect your data using industry-standard security measures. Since the Service is available globally, your information may be processed and stored on servers located outside of your home country, subject to appropriate security safeguards.
